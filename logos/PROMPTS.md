# Image-generation prompt set

Mode: Codex built-in `image_gen`, one call per option. Each call used the creator's image as a visual reference, after inspecting it. Each requested one 1024 × 1024 opaque square picture, an original generic digital pet, no brands or existing characters, and the reference's green, mint and near-black colors. This file records the design directions used for the final five images.

1. **Mascot:** Preserve the friendly green square digital pet, stubby ear protrusions, black square eyes with cream highlights and little black smile. Reimagine just the face with a chunky dark outline and deliberately stepped corners. Large central head, simple eyes and broad smiling mouth. Pale mint background extending to every edge. No handheld console, outer frame, coin rim, words, letters, numbers, additional objects or fine textures.
2. **Geometric:** Abstract the pet into one squat deep-green square silhouette with two short squared ear bumps, two square mint cutout eyes and a thick mint smiling notch. Broad head, big eyes, tiny confident smile. Pale mint background covering the square. Keep the ears and chin within a 32-pixel circular crop. Minimal graphic treatment, no scene, console, text, border or perimeter ornament.
3. **Lettermark:** The only text is exactly **SFPT**, arranged **SF** above **PT** in a compact bespoke pixel-block monogram. Equal-weight, substantial custom block letters with wide open counters. Ear-like protrusions integrated into the upper silhouettes. Near-black letters, a fresh-green offset edge and a full mint background. Legible at 64 pixels; no extra wording, frame, gadgets or scene.
4. **Badge:** A straight-on jade-green minted token with a large raised relief of the reference's pet face. Two squared ear nubs, large dark eyes and a broad small smile. One dark evergreen rim and restrained mint bevel, fresh-green jade surfaces and carved shadows. More rounded and embossed than the pixel original; readable at 64 pixels. Mint background, no writing, numerals, tiny engraving, stars, extra coins or scene.
5. **Meme:** One huge, goofy, ecstatic square pet bust leaning forward diagonally, with large shining square eyes, an exaggerated delighted grin and two tiny clenched mitten fists beside its cheeks. Thick near-black ink contours, fresh-green skin, emerald shadows and mint highlights. One pale-mint radial comic burst on a deep-green full background. Keep the face dominant at 64 pixels. No text, sound effects, props, borders, extra characters or fine marks.
