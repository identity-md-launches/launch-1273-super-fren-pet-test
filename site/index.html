02 / Built into the code

Less to trust.
More to understand.

One billion. Once.

All 1,000,000,000 SFPT are minted to the deployer at creation. The supply has 18 decimals and can never grow.

Fixed supply

A place for the swarm.

The launch factory assigns 10% through its Merkle distributor. The other 90% is budgeted to seed the SFPT / IMD pool.

90% pool / 10% swarm

The fee has one route.

The 2% buy tax goes to one treasury in SFPT. Sells, seed deposits and ordinary wallet transfers have no token tax.

Fixed rate / fixed recipient

No control room.

No owner, admin, upgrade switch or ability to change fees. The treasury and tax rate are constants in the token’s code.

No privileged controls

07 / A few good questions

Get to know your fren.

Is this Super Fren Pet or Super Fren Pet Test?+

This launch is Super Fren Pet Test, ticker SFPT. The creator’s pet-room concept originally used SFP. The room carries that idea, but all token details on this site refer to SFPT. The creator says: “Do not buy, this is a test.”

Does every trade feed the pet?+

That phrase describes the room’s creative concept, not an on-chain guarantee. The implemented contract sends a fixed 2% of outgoing PoolManager transfers to the treasury in SFPT. It does not convert to IMD or FP, bridge to Base, or stake into a pet.

What fees apply when buying or selling?+

Buying SFPT from the PoolManager incurs a 2% token tax, rounded down to token minor units. Selling into the PoolManager has no token tax. The Uniswap v4 pool has a separate 1.25% fee, and gas and price impact may also apply. Other PoolManager outflows, including liquidity withdrawals, are subject to the token’s same outgoing-transfer rule.

Can anyone mint more or change the treasury?+

No. All one billion SFPT are minted once to the deployer. There is no owner or administrative function, and both the 2% rate and treasury wallet are constants.

Why are the price and address blank?+

The site loads launch information from its same-origin data feed. Until an address and market values arrive, it shows “launching…” and dashes. If a refresh fails, market values are cleared and the last confirmed links and address, if any, are labeled as cached.

Is the pet room a game or a wallet?+

It is an interactive illustration. “Trace a buy” demonstrates the token split without moving funds. The pet HUD, bridge window, leaderboard and robot keeper are concept scenery. Nothing connects to your wallet or records personal information.
